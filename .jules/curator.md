## 2024-05-24 - Fix Type Instability in List Column Initialization
**Learning:** Initializing data.frame columns using `c(1:cross_units)` and then attempting to assign lists iteratively (`df$target[i] <- list(...)`) works conceptually, but can either silently coerce elements or fail depending on R version/configuration (and often warns or raises `replacement has length > 1`). In this codebase, lists are assigned iteratively in `R/AWS/json.Rmd`, `R/aws_prep.Rmd`, `R/AWS/sagemaker.R`, and `R/AWS/sagemaker_run.R`.
**Action:** Replace `target = c(1:cross_units)` with `target = I(vector("list", cross_units))` to correctly instantiate a list-column. Replace `df$target[i] <- list(...)` with `df$target[[i]] <- ...` to properly assign individual elements in the list-column without implicit conversions or length mismatch issues.
